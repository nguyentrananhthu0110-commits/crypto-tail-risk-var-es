CRYPTO RISK INTELLIGENCE - CACH CHAY KHONG CAN CMD
==================================================

Ban ZIP nay da dong goi SAN du lieu va mo hinh tham chieu.
Vi vay lan dau ban khong bat buoc phai nap du lieu ngay.

CACH DE NHAT (khuyen nghi)
--------------------------
1. Giai nen toan bo ZIP (Extract All). Khong chay file ben trong man hinh xem ZIP.
2. Mo thu muc crypto-risk-intelligence.
3. Double-click START_HERE.pyw.
4. Bam "1) Cai dat thu vien" va doi hoan tat.
5. Bam "3) Mo ung dung". Trinh duyet se mo http://127.0.0.1:8765.

Neu double-click START_HERE.pyw lai mo VS Code/Notepad:
- Nhap chuot phai vao START_HERE.pyw -> Open with -> Python; hoac
- Mo PowerShell trong thu muc nay va chay:  py .\START_HERE.pyw

NAP DU LIEU MOI
---------------
Co 3 cach, chon 1 cach:
A. Trong START_HERE.pyw: bam "2) Nap du lieu & huan luyen" hoac "Cap nhat du lieu".
B. Trong trinh duyet: mo tab "Du lieu & he thong" -> bam "Nap du lieu & huan luyen".
C. Bang PowerShell:
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\setup.ps1
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\prepare_data.ps1
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\run_app.ps1

Neu dang dung VS Code:
Mo thu muc crypto-risk-intelligence -> Terminal -> Run Task -> chon cac task "CRI: ...".

Cac file .cmd van duoc giu lam phuong an phu; ban khong can dung chung.
